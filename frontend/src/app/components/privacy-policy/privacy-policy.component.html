Privacy Policy

Block Master is self-hosted. It runs on its owner's node and talks to that node's Bitcoin Core and Electrum server.

  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party trackers.
  • Your settings (language, currency, theme, holiday theme) are stored only in your browser's local storage.
  • The node owner's web server may keep standard access logs, for example of which pages were requested.
  • If the price feed is enabled, the backend fetches exchange rates from public price APIs. It does not send your addresses or transactions anywhere.
  • Looking up an address or transaction here only queries this node, not an outside server.
