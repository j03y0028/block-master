Block Master is a friendly, self-hosted Bitcoin explorer for your own node.

See what is waiting to be mined, what fee to pay, and every block and transaction, straight from your own Bitcoin node. No accounts, no tracking, no third-party servers. Fee advice is written in plain language, and the logo dresses up for U.S. holidays.

The mempool

The waiting room for transactions. When you send bitcoin, it waits here until a miner includes it in a block.

Blocks

Roughly every 10 minutes a miner adds a block, a batch of transactions that becomes a permanent page of the ledger.

Fees

Block space is limited, so miners take the highest fee rate (sat/vB) first. Busy waiting room = higher fees.

Holiday themes

Around New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Halloween, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas, the Block Master logo and accent colours change automatically, based on your local date. Choose a specific theme or turn it off with the Holiday theme menu at the bottom of every page.
